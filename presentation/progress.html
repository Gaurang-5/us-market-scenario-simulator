Project goal

One question, clear assumptions

“Oil rises 10%. The US 10-year yield rises 20 basis points. What could happen over 5 trading days?”
01

Read the scenario

Extract stated numbers and ask for missing details.

02

Estimate ranges

Use a numerical model for SPY and five sector ETFs.

03

Show the limits

Display the assumptions, horizon, and uncertainty.

The output is a conditional scenario estimate. It does not predict whether the event will occur.
